1. Welcome to Merchant Portal 2.0

The PayFast Merchant Portal is your single place to monitor payments, process refunds, review settlements, raise complaints and manage who in your business can access your account.

Merchant Portal 2.0 brings a refreshed, easier-to-use design. Everything you relied on before is still here, it is simply faster to find and clearer to read.

What’s new

  • A dashboard at a glance: see settled, unsettled and failed transactions, channel performance and open requests as soon as you sign in.

  • Simpler navigation: related screens are grouped into menus, so fewer clicks get you where you need to go.

  • Clearer search: larger filters and a cleaner layout on transaction, refund and settlement screens.

  • Better complaint handling: raise and follow up on complaints through a message thread, with file attachments.

  • Easier user management: create users and assign role-based permissions in a few steps.

  • Notifications: stay informed with in-portal notifications for new tickets, responses from the Premier PayFast team on login-related queries, and any changes made.

  • AI Support: get instant assistance through the AI Support icon, available at the top right of the screen in the merchant portal.

2. Signing In

Use the username (or e-mail address) and password provided to you when your account was set up.

  1. Open the Merchant Portal using the link shared with you by PayFast.

  2. Enter your Username or Email.

  3. Enter your Password.

  4. Click LOGIN. Your Dashboard opens automatically.

Figure 1: Merchant Portal sign-in screen

Forgot your password?

  1. On the sign-in screen, click Forgot Password?

  2. Enter your registered e-mail address and submit.

  3. Open the e-mail you receive and follow the link to set a new password.

  4. Return to the sign-in screen and log in with your new password.

TIP

Didn’t receive the reset e-mail within a few minutes? Check your spam or junk folder before contacting support.

3. Finding Your Way Around

Every screen in the portal follows the same layout, so once you know it you can find anything quickly.

Figure 2: Portal layout — header across the top, navigation menu on the left, workspace in the centre

The header

The header runs across the top of every screen. Use it to switch between the PayFast services on your account:

Service What you can do
OneCheckout Your main online payment workspace - transactions, refunds, settlements, complaints, outlets and users.
RaastPay Create and view QR codes, track QR payments and manage QR refund requests.
PayLinks Track payments collected through PayLinks and manage your payment links.

You will only see the services that are enabled on your account. The icons on the right of the header give you access to notifications and your profile.

The navigation menu

The menu on the left groups related screens together. Click a heading to expand it and see the screens inside. The menu for OneCheckout contains:

  • Dashboard: your account overview

  • Transactions: live and past transactions, payment notifications and capture/void

  • Refund: refundable transactions, past refundables, refund requests and adjustments

  • Settlement Details: settlement summary, settlement details and past settlements

  • Hold & Chargeback Requests: transactions on hold and chargeback cases

  • Outlet Management: your stores and outlets

  • Complaint Management: view and create complaints

  • About You: your profile and contact details

  • User Management: View/Create users and user groups

4. Dashboard

The Dashboard opens as soon as you sign in and gives you a live summary of your account. You can return to it at any time from Dashboard in the menu.

Choose a time period

Use the filter in the top-right corner to view figures for Today, Last 7 Days or Last 30 Days. All cards on the Dashboard update to match.

Transaction summary

  • Settled Transaction Sales: the value of transactions already settled to your account.

  • Unsettled Transaction Sales: successful transactions awaiting settlement.

  • Total Transactions: how many transactions were attempted, the value of successful sales and your success rate.

  • Failed Transactions: a chart of failed transactions grouped by reason, so you can spot issues early and act quickly.

Transaction overview by channel

See the number and value of transactions for each payment channel. Select Card, Wallet, Bank or Raast to see the breakdown for that channel — for example, Visa, Mastercard, PayPak and UnionPay under Card.

Report links, requests and tickets

Figure 3: Report Links, Requests Overview and the channel breakdown

  • Report Links: one-click shortcuts to Settlement Summary, Transaction Details and Refund Requests.

  • Requests Overview: the number of open Refund Requests and Chargebacks.

  • Ticket Summary: a count of your complaints by type (chargeback, refund, settlement, merchant account, hold and transaction). Click View All to open your complaints list.

5. Transactions

The Transactions menu brings together everything you need to look up and manage individual payments.

Screen Use it to
Live Transactions Search your recent transactions.
Past Transactions Search older transactions that are no longer shown under Live Transactions.
Payment Notifications Review the payment notifications (IPN) sent to your website or system for each transaction — useful if your system did not receive a payment confirmation.
Capture/Void Transactions Capture authorised card payments to collect funds, or void them to cancel before settlement. Available if this service is enabled on your account.

Finding a transaction

  1. Go to Transactions and select Live Transactions (or Past Transactions for older records).

  2. Click Search Filters and enter your criteria; for example, a date range, Order ID or Transaction ID.

  3. For more options, click Show More Filters.

  4. Click Search. Matching transactions appear in the table below.

  5. To keep a copy, click the CSV icon above the table to download the results.

Figure 4: Live Transactions

Each row shows the date and time, Order ID, Transaction ID, amount, issuer, status, type, response message and settlement status.

Figure 5: Past Transactions

TIP

Searching by Order ID (your own reference) or Transaction ID (PayFast’s reference) is the fastest way to find a specific payment.

6. Refunds

All refund activity is grouped under the Refund menu.

Screen Use it to
Refundable Transactions Find eligible transactions and start a refund.
Past Refundables View refunds that have already been completed.
Refund Requests Review and track refund requests.
Refund Adjustment View refund adjustments applied to your account.

How to refund a transaction?

  1. Go to Refund › Refundable Transactions.

  2. Click Search Filters, select a date range and click Search.

  3. Find the transaction in the list. Click Details to check its information before refunding.

  4. Click Refund on that transaction and confirm the refund.

  5. Track progress under Refund Requests. Once completed, the refund appears under Past Refundables.

Figure 6: Refundable Transactions - search screen

Figure 7: Refundable Transactions - use Details to review and Refund to start a refund

IMPORTANT: Check before you refund

Always confirm the Order ID, amount and customer details before starting a refund. If a transaction is not listed as refundable, or you need help with a refund, raise a complaint (see Section 10) or contact support.

10. Complaint Management

Raise an issue with PayFast, follow its progress and message our team — all from inside the portal.

Viewing your complaints

  1. Go to Complaint Management › View Complaints.

  2. Your recent complaints are listed with their ID, status, type, title and dates.

  3. Use Search Filters to narrow the list by Status, Ticket Type, Ticket ID or date range.

  4. Click View on any complaint to open its details.

Figure 12: View Complaints with search filters

Following up on a complaint

When you open a complaint, its details appear on the left and a Communication Thread appears on the right. Type your message in the box, attach files if needed, and click Send Message. Our team’s replies appear in the same thread. Click Back to return to your list.

Figure 13: Complaint details and communication thread

Raising a new complaint

  1. Go to Complaint Management › View Complaints and click Create Ticket (top right).

  2. Select the Complaint Type that best describes your issue (see the table below).

  3. Enter the Transaction ID or Order ID of the affected transaction.

  4. Enter a short Complaint Title.

  5. Describe the issue in Message (up to 1,024 characters).

  6. Optionally, attach supporting files such as screenshots, Excel or Word documents — up to 5 files, 2 MB each.

  7. Click Create Complaint. Your complaint appears in View Complaints, where you can track it.

Figure 14: Create a Complaint form

Choosing the right complaint type

Complaint type Use it for
Refund Dispute Issues with a refund you have made or requested.
Settlement Dispute Missing, delayed or incorrect settlement amounts.
Transaction Configuration Dispute Problems with how payments or payment channels are set up on your account.
Merchant Account Dispute Questions about your merchant account or profile.
Other Dispute Anything that doesn’t fit the categories above.

Figure 15: Complaint Type options

TIP: Get a faster resolution

Include the transaction date, amount and Order ID in your message.

Attach a screenshot of any error you saw.

Raise one complaint per issue so each can be tracked and resolved separately.

12. User Management

Give each member of your team their own login and control exactly what they can see and do.

Screen Use it to
View Users See everyone who has access to your account. Click Edit on a user to update their details.
Create User Add a new team member.
View User Groups See the groups of permissions you have set up.
Create User Group Create a role with a specific set of permissions.

Figure 17: View Users

Creating a user group

A user group is a role such as “Finance” or “Support” with its own set of permissions. Set up groups first, then assign users to them.

  1. Go to User Management › Create User Group.

  2. Enter a Group Title (letters, numbers and hyphens only) and a short Description.

  3. Under Permissions, expand each area and select what this group can access.

  4. Switch the group to Active and click Submit.

Figure 18: Create User Group with permissions

Adding a user

  1. Go to User Management › Create User.

  2. Enter the user’s details and e-mail address, and choose their user group.

  3. Save. The new user receives an e-mail with their login details and can sign in straight away.

TIP : Give only the access people need

Assign each user the smallest set of permissions they need for their job. For example, a support agent may need Complaint Management but not settlement or user-management access.

15. Keeping Your Account Secure

Your portal gives access to financial information and actions such as refunds. Follow these practices to keep it safe:

  • Keep your password private. Do not share it with anyone, including colleagues.

  • One person, one login. Create a separate user for each team member instead of sharing a single account.

  • Limit access. Use user groups so each person can only reach the screens they need.

  • Remove access promptly. Update or deactivate a user as soon as they change roles or leave your business.

  • Sign out on shared devices and never save your password on a public or shared computer.

  • Be alert to suspicious messages. If you receive an unexpected request for your login details, or notice activity you don’t recognise, contact PayFast support immediately at: 021-37133278

16. Glossary

Term Meaning
Chargeback A payment disputed by a cardholder through their bank, which may result in the amount being reversed.
Hold A transaction temporarily held pending review.
IBAN International Bank Account Number — the 24-character format used for bank accounts in Pakistan.
IPN Instant Payment Notification — a message PayFast sends to your website or system to confirm a payment’s status.
MDR Merchant Discount Rate — the processing fee applied to transactions, as agreed with PayFast.
Order ID Your own reference for a payment, set by your website or system.
Raast Pakistan’s instant payment system, used by RaastPay for QR payments.
Refund Returning all or part of a payment to your customer.
Settled / Unsettled Settled funds have been paid into your bank account; unsettled funds are from successful transactions still awaiting settlement.
Transaction ID PayFast’s unique reference for a transaction.
